Causation and confounding因果與干擾

An association becomes interesting when we can argue it is causal. This module defines a causal effect, shows how confounding breaks the link between association and causation, and introduces the tools used to repair it.

當我們能論證關聯具因果性時，關聯才真正有意義。本單元定義因果效應，說明干擾如何破壞關聯與因果之間的連結，並介紹修正的方法。

What is a causal effect?什麼是因果效應？

For each person imagine two potential outcomes: \(Y^{a=1}\), the outcome if exposed, and \(Y^{a=0}\), the outcome if unexposed. The exposure has a causal effect for that person if the two differ. We only ever observe one of them, so individual effects are unknowable, but average effects in a population are not:

想像每個人都有兩個潛在結果：曝露時的結果 \(Y^{a=1}\) 與未曝露時的結果 \(Y^{a=0}\)。兩者不同，即表示曝露對此人有因果效應。我們只能觀察到其中之一，因此個人效應無法得知，但族群的平均效應可以估計：

$$\text{causal RR} = \frac{\Pr[Y^{a=1} = 1]}{\Pr[Y^{a=0} = 1]}$$

The observed RR compares two different groups of people. It equals the causal RR only if the unexposed group shows what would have happened to the exposed group without exposure. This condition is called exchangeability.

觀察到的 RR 比較的是兩群不同的人。只有當未曝露組能代表曝露組在未曝露時的情況，觀察 RR 才等於因果 RR，此條件稱為可交換性。

Confounding干擾

Confounding is the lack of exchangeability caused by a common cause of exposure and outcome. The traditional checklist says a confounder is associated with the exposure, is a risk factor for the outcome, and is not on the causal pathway. The checklist is a useful start but can mislead; a causal diagram is more reliable.

干擾是曝露與結果的共同原因所造成的不可交換性。傳統準則認為干擾因子須與曝露相關、為結果的危險因子，且不在因果路徑上。此準則可作為起點，但可能誤導；因果圖較為可靠。

The backdoor criterion, informally後門準則（簡述）

Adjust for a set of variables that blocks every path from exposure to outcome that starts with an arrow pointing into the exposure, without adjusting for descendants of the exposure. Draw the DAG before looking at the data.

選擇一組變項加以調整，使其阻斷所有以箭頭指向曝露開始的路徑，且不調整曝露的下游變項。請在看資料之前先畫出 DAG。

Controlling confounding控制干擾

At the design stage: randomization, restriction (study only one level of the confounder), and matching. At the analysis stage: stratification with Mantel–Haenszel pooling, standardization, regression, and propensity score methods. Every analytic method only handles confounders that were measured, and measured well.

設計階段：隨機分派、限制（只研究干擾因子的某一層級）與配對。分析階段：分層並以 Mantel–Haenszel 法合併、標準化、迴歸與傾向分數方法。所有分析方法都只能處理已測量且測量良好的干擾因子。

$$\text{OR}_{\text{MH}} = \frac{\sum_i a_i d_i / n_i}{\sum_i b_i c_i / n_i}$$

A weighted average of the stratum-specific odds ratios, where \(i\) indexes strata of the confounder.

各層勝算比的加權平均，\(i\) 代表干擾因子的各層。

Effect modification效應修飾

Effect modification means the effect of the exposure differs across levels of a third variable. Unlike confounding, it is not a bias to remove but a finding to report. It is also scale-dependent: an effect can be constant on the ratio scale while varying on the difference scale.

效應修飾指曝露的效應在第三變項的不同層級間有所差異。與干擾不同，它不是需要移除的偏差，而是應報告的發現。效應修飾也依賴尺度：效應可能在比值尺度上恆定，在差值尺度上卻不同。

For public health, interaction on the additive scale is usually more relevant because it identifies where an intervention prevents the most cases. With two binary exposures and the doubly unexposed group as reference, the relative excess risk due to interaction is:

在公共衛生上，加法尺度的交互作用通常較具意義，因為它能指出介入可預防最多病例之處。以兩個二元曝露、雙重未曝露組為參考組，交互作用相對超額風險為：

$$\text{RERI} = \text{RR}_{11} - \text{RR}_{10} - \text{RR}_{01} + 1$$

RERI > 0 indicates positive additive interaction: the joint effect exceeds the sum of the separate effects.

RERI > 0 表示正向加法交互作用：共同效應大於個別效應之和。

Judging causality因果判斷

Bradford Hill's nine viewpoints (strength, consistency, specificity, temporality, biological gradient, plausibility, coherence, experiment, and analogy) are aids to judgement, not a checklist. Only temporality is required: the cause must precede the effect.

Bradford Hill 的九項觀點（強度、一致性、特異性、時序性、劑量效應、合理性、連貫性、實驗證據與類比）是輔助判斷的工具，而非檢核表。唯一必要的是時序性：原因必須先於結果。

Check your understanding自我檢測

Q1. You study maternal pre-pregnancy BMI and infant birth weight. Should you adjust for gestational diabetes?Q1. 研究孕前 BMI 與新生兒出生體重時，應調整妊娠糖尿病嗎？

Not if you want the total effect. GDM is caused by high BMI and affects birth weight, so it is a mediator. Adjusting for it estimates something closer to a direct effect, and can introduce collider bias if GDM and birth weight share unmeasured causes.

若欲估計總效應則不應調整。GDM 由高 BMI 導致並影響出生體重，屬於中介因子。調整後估計的較接近直接效應；若 GDM 與出生體重有未測量的共同原因，還可能引入碰撞偏差。

Q2. RR for smoking alone = 2, for asbestos alone = 5, for both = 10. Is there interaction?Q2. 單獨吸菸 RR = 2，單獨石綿 RR = 5，兩者兼具 RR = 10。是否有交互作用？

On the multiplicative scale, no: 2 × 5 = 10. On the additive scale, yes: RERI = 10 − 2 − 5 + 1 = 4. The same data show interaction on one scale and not the other, which is why you must state the scale.

乘法尺度上沒有：2 × 5 = 10。加法尺度上有：RERI = 10 − 2 − 5 + 1 = 4。同一資料在一種尺度有交互作用、另一種沒有，因此必須說明所用尺度。
