Screening and diagnostic tests篩檢與診斷檢驗

A test's accuracy is a property of the test. What a positive result means for the person in front of you depends just as much on who is being tested.

檢驗的準確度是檢驗本身的特性；但陽性結果對眼前這個人代表什麼，同樣取決於受檢的是哪些人。

After this module you can學習目標

  • calculate sensitivity, specificity, predictive values, and likelihood ratios;
  • explain why predictive values change with prevalence;
  • recognize lead-time bias, length-time bias, and overdiagnosis.
  • 計算敏感度、特異度、預測值與概似比；
  • 說明預測值為何隨盛行率改變；
  • 辨識領先時間偏差、病程長短偏差與過度診斷。

Sensitivity and specificity are read down the columns: they condition on true disease status. Predictive values are read across the rows: they condition on the test result, which is what a clinician actually knows.

敏感度與特異度沿欄計算，以真實疾病狀態為條件。預測值沿列計算，以檢驗結果為條件，這才是臨床人員實際掌握的資訊。

Why prevalence matters盛行率的影響

By Bayes' theorem, the positive predictive value combines test accuracy with the prior probability of disease:

依貝氏定理，陽性預測值結合了檢驗準確度與疾病的事前機率：

$$\text{PPV} = \frac{\text{Se} \times P}{\text{Se} \times P + (1 - \text{Sp})(1 - P)}$$

When prevalence is low, even a small false-positive rate applied to the large healthy majority produces more false positives than true positives. Set prevalence to 1% below and compare it with a specialist clinic where it is 20%.

盛行率低時，即使偽陽性率很小，套用在龐大的健康族群上，偽陽性仍會多於真陽性。請將下方盛行率設為 1%，再與盛行率 20% 的專科門診比較。

Likelihood ratios概似比

Likelihood ratios summarize a test independently of prevalence and update a pre-test probability directly: post-test odds = pre-test odds × LR.

概似比不受盛行率影響，可直接更新檢驗前機率：檢驗後勝算 = 檢驗前勝算 × 概似比。

$$\text{LR}^{+} = \frac{\text{Se}}{1 - \text{Sp}} \qquad \text{LR}^{-} = \frac{1 - \text{Se}}{\text{Sp}}$$

An LR+ above 10 or an LR− below 0.1 changes the probability of disease substantially.

LR+ 大於 10 或 LR− 小於 0.1 時，會大幅改變罹病機率。

Choosing a cut-off選擇切點

For a continuous marker such as fasting glucose, moving the cut-off trades sensitivity for specificity. The receiver operating characteristic (ROC) curve plots sensitivity against 1 − specificity across all cut-offs; the area under it summarizes discrimination. The best cut-off depends on the relative costs of missed cases and false alarms, not on the curve alone.

對空腹血糖等連續型指標而言，移動切點即是在敏感度與特異度之間取捨。接收者操作特徵（ROC）曲線描繪所有切點下敏感度對 1 − 特異度的關係，曲線下面積概括鑑別力。最佳切點取決於漏診與偽陽性的相對代價，而非僅由曲線決定。

Evaluating screening programmes評估篩檢計畫

Comparing survival between screen-detected and clinically detected cases is misleading because of three biases:

比較篩檢發現與臨床發現病例的存活時間會產生誤導，原因在於三種偏差：

  • Lead-time bias. Earlier diagnosis lengthens measured survival even if death occurs at the same time.
  • Length-time bias. Slow-growing disease spends longer in the detectable preclinical phase, so screening preferentially finds cases with better prognosis.
  • Overdiagnosis. Some screen-detected disease would never have caused symptoms in the person's lifetime.
  • 領先時間偏差。即使死亡時間相同，較早診斷也會延長測得的存活時間。
  • 病程長短偏差。進展緩慢的疾病停留在可偵測臨床前期的時間較長，因此篩檢較常找到預後較佳的病例。
  • 過度診斷。部分篩檢發現的疾病終其一生都不會造成症狀。

The unbiased comparison is disease-specific mortality in randomized screened and unscreened populations.

無偏差的比較方式，是在隨機分派的篩檢與未篩檢族群中比較疾病別死亡率。

Check your understanding自我檢測

Q1. A test has Se = 90% and Sp = 95%. In a population with 1% prevalence, what fraction of positives are true positives?Q1. 某檢驗敏感度 90%、特異度 95%。在盛行率 1% 的族群中，陽性結果有多少比例是真陽性？

Per 10,000 people: 100 diseased, 90 test positive; 9,900 healthy, 495 test positive. PPV = 90 / 585 = 15.4%. About 5 in 6 positives are false.

每 10,000 人：100 人有病，90 人陽性；9,900 人無病，495 人陽性。PPV = 90 / 585 = 15.4%，約每 6 個陽性中有 5 個是偽陽性。

Q2. Calculate LR+ for that test and the post-test probability for someone with a 20% pre-test probability.Q2. 計算該檢驗的 LR+，以及檢驗前機率 20% 者的檢驗後機率。

LR+ = 0.90 / 0.05 = 18. Pre-test odds = 0.2 / 0.8 = 0.25. Post-test odds = 0.25 × 18 = 4.5, so probability = 4.5 / 5.5 = 82%.

LR+ = 0.90 / 0.05 = 18。檢驗前勝算 = 0.2 / 0.8 = 0.25；檢驗後勝算 = 0.25 × 18 = 4.5，機率 = 4.5 / 5.5 = 82%。
