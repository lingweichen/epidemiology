Bias偏差

A larger sample shrinks random error. It does nothing for systematic error. Bias is any systematic deviation of an estimate from the truth, and it comes from how participants were selected or how information about them was obtained.

增加樣本數能縮小隨機誤差，但對系統性誤差毫無幫助。偏差是估計值與真值之間的系統性差異，來自參與者的選取方式或資訊的取得方式。

After this module you can學習目標

  • distinguish random error from systematic error, and internal from external validity;
  • recognize selection bias in case-control and cohort studies;
  • predict the direction of bias from non-differential and differential misclassification.
  • 區分隨機誤差與系統性誤差，以及內在效度與外在效度；
  • 辨識病例對照與世代研究中的選樣偏差；
  • 預測非差異性與差異性錯誤分類造成的偏差方向。

Validity效度

Internal validity asks whether the estimate is correct for the people studied. External validity (generalizability) asks whether it applies to other populations. Internal validity comes first: an invalid estimate cannot be generalized.

內在效度指估計值對研究對象是否正確；外在效度（概化性）指能否推論到其他族群。內在效度優先：無效的估計值無從推論。

The three main threats to internal validity are selection bias, information bias, and confounding. Confounding has its own module.

內在效度的三大威脅為選樣偏差、資訊偏差與干擾。干擾另於第 5 單元說明。

Selection bias選樣偏差

Selection bias arises when the probability of being included in the analysis depends on both exposure and outcome. Common forms:

當進入分析的機率同時與曝露及結果有關時，便產生選樣偏差。常見形式：

  • Control selection. Hospital controls admitted for conditions related to the exposure distort the comparison. Smoking is over-represented among patients admitted for respiratory disease.
  • Differential loss to follow-up. In a cohort, if exposed participants who develop the outcome drop out more often, the observed risk in the exposed is too low.
  • Healthy worker effect. Employed people are healthier than the general population, so occupational cohorts compared with national rates look protected.
  • Self-selection. Volunteers differ from non-volunteers in health and behaviour.
  • 對照選擇。若醫院對照因與曝露相關的疾病住院，比較便會失真。例如呼吸道疾病住院病人中吸菸者比例偏高。
  • 差異性失去追蹤。世代研究中，若發生結果的曝露者較常退出，曝露組觀察風險會偏低。
  • 健康工人效應。就業者較一般族群健康，職業世代與全國率比較時會顯得具保護作用。
  • 自我選擇。自願參與者在健康與行為上與非參與者不同。

Information bias資訊偏差

Information bias comes from errors in measuring exposure, outcome, or covariates. For categorical variables these errors are called misclassification, and the key question is whether the error is the same across the groups being compared.

資訊偏差來自曝露、結果或共變項的測量錯誤。類別變項的測量錯誤稱為錯誤分類，關鍵在於比較組間錯誤是否相同。

Definition定義Usual direction通常方向
Non-differential非差異性 Exposure is misclassified equally in cases and non-cases曝露在病例與非病例中錯誤分類程度相同 Toward the null, for a binary exposure二元曝露時趨向虛無值
Differential差異性 Accuracy differs between cases and non-cases病例與非病例的準確度不同 Either direction任一方向

Recall bias is the classic differential error: mothers of infants with a birth defect search their memories harder for exposures during pregnancy than mothers of healthy infants.

回憶偏差是典型的差異性錯誤：出生缺陷兒的母親比健康嬰兒的母親更努力回想孕期曝露。

"Toward the null" has conditions「趨向虛無值」有前提

The rule holds on average for a binary exposure with errors independent of other errors. With three or more exposure categories, correlated errors, or in any single study by chance, non-differential misclassification can bias away from the null. A null result from a poorly measured exposure is not reassuring.

此規則在二元曝露且錯誤彼此獨立時，平均而言成立。若曝露有三個以上類別、錯誤彼此相關，或在單一研究中因機遇，非差異性錯誤分類仍可能使估計遠離虛無值。測量不佳的曝露所得的無關聯結果，並不能令人放心。

Reducing bias減少偏差

Bias is mostly prevented at the design stage: define the source population clearly, sample controls from it, minimize loss to follow-up, use validated and objective measurements, and blind interviewers and outcome assessors to exposure. When some bias is unavoidable, quantitative bias analysis estimates how large it would need to be to explain the result.

偏差主要在設計階段預防：明確定義來源族群並從中抽取對照、減少失去追蹤、使用經驗證的客觀測量，並讓訪員與結果評估者不知曉曝露狀態。若偏差無法避免，可用量化偏差分析評估偏差需多大才能解釋研究結果。

Check your understanding自我檢測

Q1. A cohort measures habitual diet with a food frequency questionnaire before any outcome occurs. Is misclassification of diet likely to be differential?Q1. 某世代於任何結果發生前以食物頻率問卷測量平時飲食。飲食的錯誤分類可能是差異性的嗎？

Probably not. Because diet was recorded before outcomes, the error cannot depend on outcome status. The error is likely non-differential, which for a binary exposure tends to attenuate associations.

可能性低。飲食在結果發生前記錄，錯誤不會受結果狀態影響，因此可能為非差異性，二元曝露時傾向減弱關聯。

Q2. Why does a larger sample size not fix selection bias?Q2. 為何增加樣本數無法解決選樣偏差？

A larger sample gives a more precise estimate of the same biased quantity. The confidence interval narrows around the wrong value.

樣本數增加只會讓同一個有偏差的量估計得更精確，信賴區間在錯誤的數值周圍變窄。
