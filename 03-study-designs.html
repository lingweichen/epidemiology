Study designs研究設計

Every design is a strategy for comparing the experience of exposed and unexposed people. They differ in who is sampled, when, and therefore in which measures they can estimate.

各種研究設計都是比較曝露與未曝露者經驗的策略，差別在於抽樣對象、抽樣時間，以及因此能估計的指標。

Randomized controlled trials隨機對照試驗

The investigator assigns exposure by chance. Randomization makes the groups exchangeable in expectation, balancing both measured and unmeasured prognostic factors, which is why a well-conducted trial gives the most direct estimate of a causal effect. Analysis by intention to treat preserves that balance.

研究者以隨機方式分派曝露。隨機分派使各組在期望值上具可交換性，同時平衡已測量與未測量的預後因子，因此執行良好的試驗能最直接地估計因果效應。意向治療分析可保留此平衡。

Trials are often impossible or unethical: we cannot randomize pregnant women to smoke, and rare or long-latency outcomes need impractically large or long trials.

試驗常不可行或不合倫理：無法隨機分派孕婦吸菸；罕見或潛伏期長的結果則需要規模過大或時間過長的試驗。

Cohort studies世代研究

People are classified by exposure and followed for outcomes. Exposure precedes outcome by design, so temporality is clear, and risks, rates, and their ratios and differences can all be estimated. Several outcomes can be studied from one exposure.

依曝露狀態分組並追蹤結果。設計上曝露先於結果，時序明確，可估計風險、發生密度及其比值與差值。同一曝露可研究多種結果。

A prospective cohort collects data going forward; a retrospective (historical) cohort uses records already collected, such as Taiwan's National Health Insurance claims. Both are cohorts, because both follow people from exposure to outcome.

前瞻性世代向後收集資料；回溯性（歷史性）世代使用既有紀錄，例如臺灣全民健康保險申報資料。兩者都是世代研究，因為都從曝露追蹤至結果。

Case-control studies病例對照研究

Instead of following everyone, the investigator samples cases and a set of controls, then compares their exposure. Think of it as an efficient way to sample the cohort that gave rise to the cases (the source population or study base). Controls should represent the exposure distribution of that source population, not simply be "people without the disease".

研究者不追蹤所有人，而是抽取病例與一組對照，再比較其曝露。可將其視為對產生病例之世代（來源族群或研究基礎）的有效率抽樣。對照應反映來源族群的曝露分布，而不僅是「沒有該病的人」。

A nested case-control study draws cases and density-sampled controls from inside an existing cohort, often to measure an expensive biomarker in stored samples only for a subset.

巢式病例對照研究自既有世代中選取病例與密度抽樣的對照，常用於僅對部分人檢測儲存檢體中的昂貴生物標記。

Cross-sectional studies橫斷面研究

Exposure and outcome are measured at the same time, giving prevalence and the prevalence ratio or prevalence odds ratio. Two problems limit causal interpretation: it is often unclear which came first, and prevalent cases over-represent people with long disease duration (module 1).

同時測量曝露與結果，可得盛行率及盛行率比或盛行勝算比。兩個問題限制因果推論：常無法確定何者在先；盛行病例過度代表病程長者（見第 1 單元）。

Ecological studies生態研究

The unit of analysis is a group, such as a county or country. They are cheap and good for generating hypotheses, but associations at the group level need not hold for individuals: the ecological fallacy.

分析單位為群體，如縣市或國家。成本低、適合產生假說，但群體層次的關聯未必適用於個人，此即生態謬誤。

Check your understanding自我檢測

Q1. You want to study whether a common prenatal supplement is associated with a rare congenital anomaly (about 1 per 2,000 births). Which design, and what is its main threat?Q1. 欲研究常見孕期補充劑與罕見先天異常（約每 2,000 名新生兒 1 例）的關聯，應採何種設計？主要威脅為何？

A case-control study, because a cohort would need tens of thousands of births to observe enough cases. The main threat is recall bias: mothers of affected infants may report supplement use more thoroughly. Using prospectively recorded prescription or pharmacy data avoids this.

病例對照研究，因為世代研究需數萬名新生兒才能觀察到足夠病例。主要威脅是回憶偏差：異常兒的母親可能更完整地回報補充劑使用。採用前瞻記錄的處方或藥局資料可避免此問題。

Q2. A study uses 2010 insurance records to identify exposure and follows patients to 2020 for outcomes. Is it a case-control study because the data are old?Q2. 某研究以 2010 年保險紀錄界定曝露，追蹤至 2020 年觀察結果。因資料是過去的，它算病例對照研究嗎？

No. It is a retrospective cohort. A design is defined by how people are sampled (here, by exposure, then followed), not by when the data were collected.

不是，它是回溯性世代研究。研究設計取決於抽樣方式（此處依曝露分組再追蹤），而非資料收集的時間。
