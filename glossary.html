Glossary名詞對照

Terms used in this course, with the Chinese equivalents used in Taiwan. Type in either language to filter.

本課程使用的術語及臺灣常用中文譯名。可輸入中文或英文篩選。


English中文Definition定義
Attributable fraction可歸因分率
Proportion of cases among the exposed that would not occur without the exposure.
曝露者病例中，若無曝露即不會發生的比例。
Bias偏差
Systematic deviation of an estimate from the true value.
估計值與真值之間的系統性差異。
Case-control study病例對照研究
Design that samples cases and controls and compares their exposure.
抽取病例與對照並比較其曝露的研究設計。
Cohort study世代研究
Design that classifies people by exposure and follows them for outcomes.
依曝露分組並追蹤結果的研究設計。
Collider碰撞因子
Variable caused by two other variables on a path; conditioning on it opens the path.
路徑上同時受兩個變項影響的變項；以其為條件會開啟該路徑。
Confounder干擾因子
Common cause of exposure and outcome that creates a non-causal association.
曝露與結果的共同原因，造成非因果關聯。
Confounding干擾
Lack of exchangeability between exposed and unexposed groups.
曝露組與未曝露組之間缺乏可交換性。
Cross-sectional study橫斷面研究
Design that measures exposure and outcome at the same time.
同時測量曝露與結果的研究設計。
Cumulative incidence (risk)累積發生率（風險）
Proportion of an initially disease-free group that develops disease over a period.
起始無病者在特定期間內發病的比例。
Directed acyclic graph (DAG)有向無環圖
Diagram of assumed causal relations with no feedback loops.
描繪假設因果關係且無回饋迴路的圖。
Ecological fallacy生態謬誤
Assuming a group-level association holds for individuals.
誤以為群體層次的關聯適用於個人。
Effect modification效應修飾
Variation in the effect of an exposure across levels of another variable.
曝露效應隨另一變項的層級而不同。
Exchangeability可交換性
The unexposed group represents what would have happened to the exposed without exposure.
未曝露組能代表曝露組在未曝露時的情況。
Exposure曝露
Factor whose effect on an outcome is being studied.
研究其對結果之效應的因素。
Incidence rate發生密度
New cases divided by person-time at risk.
新病例數除以具風險的人時。
Information bias資訊偏差
Bias from errors in measuring exposure, outcome, or covariates.
因曝露、結果或共變項測量錯誤造成的偏差。
Intention to treat意向治療分析
Analysing trial participants in the groups to which they were randomized.
依隨機分派的組別分析試驗參與者。
Lead-time bias領先時間偏差
Apparent survival gain from earlier diagnosis alone.
僅因較早診斷而產生的表面存活延長。
Likelihood ratio概似比
How much a test result changes the odds of disease.
檢驗結果使罹病勝算改變的倍數。
Mediator中介因子
Variable on the causal pathway between exposure and outcome.
位於曝露與結果因果路徑上的變項。
Misclassification錯誤分類
Placing a person in the wrong category of exposure or outcome.
將個人歸入錯誤的曝露或結果類別。
Odds ratio勝算比
Ratio of the odds of an outcome (or exposure) in two groups.
兩組結果（或曝露）勝算的比值。
Person-time人時
Sum of time each person was observed while at risk.
每人在具風險期間被觀察時間的總和。
Population attributable fraction族群可歸因分率
Proportion of all cases in a population attributable to an exposure.
族群全部病例中可歸因於某曝露的比例。
Positive predictive value陽性預測值
Probability of disease given a positive test.
檢驗陽性者真正有病的機率。
Potential outcome潛在結果
Outcome a person would have under a particular exposure value.
個人在特定曝露值下會有的結果。
Prevalence盛行率
Proportion of a population with the disease at a given time.
某時間點族群中患病者的比例。
Risk difference風險差
Risk in the exposed minus risk in the unexposed.
曝露組風險減未曝露組風險。
Risk ratio (relative risk)相對危險性
Risk in the exposed divided by risk in the unexposed.
曝露組風險除以未曝露組風險。
Selection bias選樣偏差
Bias from inclusion depending on both exposure and outcome.
進入研究的機率同時與曝露及結果相關所造成的偏差。
Sensitivity敏感度
Proportion of people with the disease who test positive.
有病者中檢驗陽性的比例。
Source population來源族群
Population that gave rise to the cases in a study.
產生研究病例的族群。
Specificity特異度
Proportion of people without the disease who test negative.
無病者中檢驗陰性的比例。
