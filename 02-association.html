Measures of association關聯性的測量

A measure of association compares disease frequency between groups. The choice between a ratio and a difference is not cosmetic: ratios describe the strength of an association, differences describe its public health impact.

關聯性指標用來比較不同群體的疾病頻率。選擇比值或差值並非形式問題：比值描述關聯強度，差值描述公共衛生影響。

After this module you can學習目標

  • calculate and interpret the risk ratio, odds ratio, and risk difference from a 2×2 table;
  • explain when the odds ratio approximates the risk ratio;

  • 由 2×2 表計算並解釋相對危險性、勝算比與風險差；

  • 計算可歸因分率並說明其背後假設。

Worked example計算範例

A hypothetical cohort follows 1,000 pregnant women: 300 with pre-pregnancy obesity (exposed) and 700 without. Gestational diabetes (GDM) develops in 45 exposed and 49 unexposed women. So a = 45, b = 255, c = 49, d = 651.

假設一世代追蹤 1,000 名孕婦：300 名孕前肥胖（曝露組），700 名非肥胖。曝露組有 45 人、未曝露組有 49 人發生妊娠糖尿病（GDM）。因此 a = 45、b = 255、c = 49、d = 651。

Ratio measures比值指標

$$\text{RR} = \frac{a/(a+b)}{c/(c+d)} = \frac{45/300}{49/700} = \frac{0.15}{0.07} = 2.14$$

The risk ratio: women with obesity had 2.14 times the risk of GDM over pregnancy.

相對危險性：肥胖孕婦在孕期發生 GDM 的風險是非肥胖孕婦的 2.14 倍。

$$\text{OR} = \frac{a/b}{c/d} = \frac{ad}{bc} = \frac{45 \times 651}{255 \times 49} = 2.34$$

The odds ratio compares odds instead of risks. It is the only ratio measure a case-control study can estimate directly (module 3).

勝算比比較的是勝算而非風險，是病例對照研究唯一能直接估計的比值指標（見第 3 單元）。

With person-time data, the rate ratio is the incidence rate in the exposed divided by the rate in the unexposed. All three ratios equal 1 when there is no association.

若為人時資料，發生密度比為曝露組發生密度除以未曝露組發生密度。無關聯時，三種比值皆等於 1。

Difference measures差值指標

$$\text{RD} = \frac{a}{a+b} - \frac{c}{c+d} = 0.15 - 0.07 = 0.08$$

The risk difference: 8 extra cases of GDM per 100 women with obesity, if the association is causal. Its null value is 0.

風險差：若關聯具因果性，每 100 名肥胖孕婦多出 8 例 GDM。虛無值為 0。

Why report both為何兩者都要報告

A risk ratio of 2 can mean 2 vs 1 per 100,000 or 40 vs 20 per 100. The relative strength is identical; the number of people affected differs by a factor of 20,000. CONSORT and STROBE both recommend reporting absolute alongside relative measures.

相對危險性為 2，可能是每 10 萬人 2 對 1，也可能是每 100 人 40 對 20。相對強度相同，受影響人數卻相差 2 萬倍。CONSORT 與 STROBE 皆建議同時報告絕對與相對指標。

When does the OR approximate the RR?勝算比何時近似相對危險性？

The two are linked exactly by the risks in each group:

兩者的關係可由各組風險精確表示：

$$\text{OR} = \text{RR} \times \frac{1 - R_0}{1 - R_1}$$

When the outcome is rare (risks under roughly 10%), both fractions are close to 1 and OR ≈ RR. When the outcome is common, the OR is always further from 1 than the RR. Reporting an OR of 3.9 as "nearly four times the risk" when the RR is 3.0 is a frequent error in the literature.

結果罕見時（風險約低於 10%），分數接近 1，OR ≈ RR。結果常見時，OR 一定比 RR 更遠離 1。文獻中常見的錯誤是：RR 為 3.0 時，將 OR 3.9 寫成「風險約為四倍」。

Attributable fractions可歸因分率

The attributable fraction among the exposed is the proportion of cases in exposed people that would not have occurred without the exposure. The population attributable fraction (PAF) is the proportion of all cases in the population that would be prevented by removing the exposure.

曝露組可歸因分率是曝露者病例中，若無曝露即不會發生的比例。族群可歸因分率（PAF）是若移除曝露，族群全部病例中可預防的比例。

$$\text{AF}_e = \frac{\text{RR} - 1}{\text{RR}} \qquad \text{PAF} = \frac{p_e(\text{RR}-1)}{1 + p_e(\text{RR}-1)} = p_c \cdot \frac{\text{RR}-1}{\text{RR}}$$

\(p_e\) is the proportion of the population exposed (Levin's formula); \(p_c\) is the proportion of cases exposed (Miettinen's formula). Use Miettinen's version when the RR is adjusted for confounders.

\(p_e\) 為族群曝露比例（Levin 公式）；\(p_c\) 為病例中曝露比例（Miettinen 公式）。若 RR 經干擾因子調整，應使用 Miettinen 公式。

Assumptions前提假設

Attributable fractions treat the association as causal and unconfounded, and assume removing the exposure is possible without changing anything else. Fractions for different risk factors of the same disease can add up to more than 100%.

可歸因分率假設關聯為因果且無干擾，並假設移除曝露時不改變其他因素。同一疾病不同危險因子的可歸因分率加總可能超過 100%。

PAF = 0.30 × 1.14 / (1 + 0.30 × 1.14) = 0.343 / 1.343 = 25.5%. Check: overall risk = 94/1000 = 9.4%, and (9.4 − 7.0)/9.4 = 25.5%.
